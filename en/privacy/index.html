Privacy policy

Ihfaz was built so that there is almost nothing to say here: the app collects no personal data. This page explains exactly what happens to your voice and to your progress.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

In short

  • No account, no sign-in.
  • Your voice is never recorded, never kept, never sent to us.
  • Your progress stays on your iPhone.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Who is responsible

Ihfaz is published by an independent developer based in Québec, Canada. A contact address will be published on this page before the app is released.

The microphone

Ihfaz asks for the microphone to hear your recitation and unveil the words you say. The microphone is on only during a test, a learning session or the microphone check of the settings, and you can turn it off at any time with the Stop button.

The sound is analysed as it arrives and then discarded. It is never written to a file, never stored, and never sent to the publisher.

Speech recognition

“Qur’an (on device)” is the engine the app uses by default. The recognition model is inside the app and runs on your iPhone: the audio does not leave the device, and it works without a network.

“Apple (Arabic, via Apple)” is a backup engine you can choose in the settings; the app also falls back on it if the on-device model cannot be loaded. It uses Apple’s speech recognition: iOS then sends the audio to Apple’s servers to transcribe it, under Apple’s privacy policy. iOS asks for your permission (“Speech Recognition”) before this can happen: without it, no audio goes to Apple.

What is stored on your iPhone

Your progress (the verses learned and tested, the sessions, the words that were not heard) and your settings (language, theme, reciter, bookmark, recent searches) are stored in the app, on your device only. The publisher has no copy and no access.

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or on a computer, this data is part of your backup, under Apple’s terms.

What goes over the network

When you ask to listen to a verse or a page, the reciter’s audio is streamed from everyayah.com. Like any server, it receives your IP address and the name of the file requested. The app sends it nothing else: no identifier, no information about you.

The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking software, and shares nothing with third parties.

Deleting your data

In the app: Settings → “Delete all my data” erases your progress and your settings from the device. Deleting the app removes everything too. There is nothing to delete elsewhere, because nothing was sent elsewhere.

Children

Ihfaz can be used at any age and collects no data from anyone, children included.

This website

This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no external fonts or scripts. It remembers the language you choose in your browser’s local storage, on your device.

It is hosted by GitHub Pages; like any host, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages (GitHub’s privacy statement).

Changes

If the app ever starts to handle data differently — an optional account to sync devices, for instance — this page will be updated before the change reaches you, and the date above will change.

Contact

A contact address will be published on this page before the app is released.
