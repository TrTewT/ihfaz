Sources and licences

Ihfaz stands on the work of people who made the text of the Qur’an, its typography and its recitations available to everyone. Here is what the app uses, and under which terms.

The text and its page

  • Tanzil Project

    Uthmani text of the Qur’an — the reference copy

    Kept without any modification; every verse of the app is checked against it when the app’s database is built.

    Licence · Verbatim copy, with attribution

  • Quranic Universal Library (Tarteel) · Quran.com

    Words, pages and lines of the fifteen-line Madinah mushaf; tajwīd annotations

    The text and the layout the app draws, word for word.

    Licence · Terms of use of Quran.com and QUL

  • DigitalKhatt Madina

    © Amine Anane, © Tarteel Inc.

    The typeface of the mushaf pages.

    Licence · SIL Open Font License 1.1

Tafsir

  • Tafsīr al-Muyassar

    King Fahd Glorious Qur’an Printing Complex — text from quranenc.com (Noor International)

    Shown in Arabic, exactly as published.

    Licence · Attribution, no modification
