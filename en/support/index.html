Getting started

How does a test work?

On the home screen, choose a surah and the part to test, then press “Start the test”. The app opens the real page of the mushaf: one verse, drawn at random, stays clear; the five that follow are blurred. Recite them from memory, out loud: each word the app hears comes out of the blur. After the five verses the app draws another starting point. “Finish” opens your summary.

How does learning work?

Choose “Learn”, then your passage. The app shows one verse. Recite it seven times while reading it: the ring moves one step each time the whole verse was heard. Then the verse hides and you recite it from memory, in three levels of less and less help. The verse is then marked as memorised and the app moves on to the next one.

What are the hizb, the rubʿ and the page on the second wheel?

They are the usual divisions of the mushaf. The Qur’an is divided into 60 hizb, each hizb into four quarters (rubʿ), and the Madinah mushaf has 604 pages. The wheel only offers the parts that belong to the surah you chose. Touch the label above the wheel to switch between them.

Where is the explanation inside the app?

The “?” beside the Test / Learn switch explains each mode in five steps. You will also find it in Settings → About → “How it works”.

The microphone and the recognition

The app does not hear me.

Check that the microphone is allowed: iPhone Settings → Ihfaz → Microphone. In a session, the indicator at the top must say “Listening…”; if the button at the bottom right says “Start”, the microphone is paused — touch it. You can also test the microphone in the app’s Settings → Speech recognition.

I said a word and it stayed blurred.

No recogniser hears everything. Recite clearly, at a steady pace, in a quiet place, with the phone close to you. If a word stays veiled, “Hint” reveals it and you can carry on: it is never counted as a mistake. A word the app did not hear is simply marked as “not heard” in the summary.

Does Ihfaz correct my tajwīd?

No. It checks which words were recited, not how they were pronounced. Judging a ghunnah or a madd from a phone’s microphone is not reliable enough to correct anyone, so the app does not claim to. The tajwīd colours are there to guide your reading; your teacher remains the reference.

Which engine should I choose?

Leave it on “Automatic”. The app then uses “Qur’an (on device)”, a model trained on Qur’anic recitation that runs on your iPhone, with nothing sent anywhere. “Apple (Arabic, via Apple)” is a backup that works through Apple’s servers.

The text and the reading

Which mushaf and which recitation?

The riwāya of Ḥafṣ ʿan ʿĀṣim, on the fifteen-line Madinah mushaf (604 pages). Other riwāyāt are not available yet.

How do I find a verse?

Touch the magnifier, on the home screen or in the reader. You can type Arabic words as you write them today, without vowels; a surah’s name (“Kahf”); a reference (“2:255”); a page (“page 50”); or a juz (“juz 30”). In the reader, “Page …” at the top lets you go to a surah, a verse or a page.

Which tafsir is shown?

Tafsīr al-Muyassar of the King Fahd Complex, in Arabic, reproduced without modification from quranenc.com. Touch a verse in the reader, then “Read the tafsir”.

I think I found a mistake in the text.

Please write to us with the surah, the verse and the page. The text comes from Quran.com and the Quranic Universal Library, is checked against the Tanzil project’s, and is never edited by hand; any report is checked against the printed mushaf.

Your data

Is my voice recorded or sent somewhere?

No. The audio is analysed as it arrives and discarded. It is never written to a file and never sent to us. See the privacy policy for the details of each engine.

Does it work without a connection?

Yes: the text, the search, the tests and the learning all work offline with the default engine. Only the reciters’ audio needs a connection, because it is streamed.

How do I start again from zero?

Settings → Privacy → “Delete all my data”. The app asks for confirmation, then erases your progress and settings from the device. This cannot be undone.

Will my progress follow me to a new iPhone?

It is part of your iPhone’s backup: if you restore the new phone from the backup of the old one, your progress comes with it. There is no account and no synchronisation in this version.
